# LiScan Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

LiScan is a 3D scanning app for iPhone and iPad devices with a LiDAR sensor.

## Data we collect

**None.** LiScan does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data or usage data.
The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## Camera and LiDAR

LiScan uses the camera and LiDAR sensor only while you are scanning, to build 3D meshes, point clouds,
room plans and object models. Camera images used for coloring scans are kept in the app's temporary
storage on your device and deleted when they are no longer needed.

## Your scans

All scans and exported files are saved only on your device, in the app's own folder
(visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › LiScan). They leave your device only when you choose
to share or export them (for example with AirDrop, Mail or iCloud Drive), in which case the app or
service you choose handles them under its own privacy policy.

Deleting a scan in LiScan, or deleting the app, permanently removes the scans from your device.

## Children

LiScan does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
